The history of this website

I have learned web development since 2020 and I have had a plan to develop my own version of social media since 2021. However, I have been busy since 2022 and did not have time to work on it, or even think about it. In 2026, many people I know advised me to open up about myself and what I have, so I decided to do so. chansol.info was created on September 3, 2026, and it is still being updated.

The philosophy about this website

This website is not related to any kinds of professionality. I have never recieved a formal education in the humanities. If you are searching for academical professionality, look elsewhere. Each category is just a topic of the pages, and it is not even perfectly corresponds. I believe creativity can come from crossing the borders. (borders between being amateur and being professional, borders between differect fields, etc.)

Creativity also comes from personal and private experiences, but I will not disclose everything. I don't like it. But I will try to be more and more open.

Just as I am not professional in Humanities, I am also not professional in English and other languages. Although I believe my English still needs to be improved, I will use it whenever I think that it would deliver my messages better. I believe that the feature of each language shapes our worldview and thus that each message could be expressed more appropriately in certain languages. Therefore, I am sparing my time on studying languages, and I will appreciate any kinds of feedback on my language usage and writing. In a same sense, it often takes too long to complete writing. So, I will just share incomplete writings in gray color like THIS.

A note about my name

My name O Chansol is written last name first, in Eastern style. So, O is my family name and Chansol is my given name. In western style, my name would be Chansol O.

Also, the official spelling of my name is Oh Chansol, not O Chansol. However, many people who know me remember it to be the latter, so I decided to follow them in informal situations. I personally don't care whether you call me O Chansol or Chansol O, O Chansol or Oh Chansol.
