Education Series (2022)

O Chansol

Reading the My Encounter with Writing part (or the entire Modern Period section) of the History of O Chansol will help you understand the Education Series much better.

The Education Series is NOT available to the public for these reasons:

  • I am dissatisfied with my writing skills.
  • It contains personal information of many people including me.


Instead, I provide its summary here.

[The "Gym Clothes" Essay] My School Should Allow Its Students to Wear Gym Clothes to and from School

It criticizes the school's violation of the students' rights.


[The "Education pt. I" Essay] Schools Should Change Their Curriculum

It argues that education should provide each student with a course suited to their academic level.


[The "Sleep Time pt. I" Essay] Is a Teenager's Sleep Time Enough? (Problem Part)

It points out that teenagers are not having enough sleep time.


[The "Education pt. II" Essay] Education: In Search of the Lost 2 Years

It investigates how the lost two years of education due to COVID-19 pandemic affected students.


[The "Sleep Time pt. II" Essay] The Effort We Need to Help Students Have Enough Sleep Time (Solution Part)

Continuing from the "Sleep Time pt. I" essay, it suggests efforts to help students have enough sleep time.


[The "Education pt. III" Essay] The Average Makers

It argues that education should give students reasons to study.


[The "Education pt. VI" Essay] Inequality; Is It a Problem with the School or the Principal?

Continuing from the "Gym Clothes" essay, it traces the origin of the suggested problem.


[The "Education pt. VI Follow-up" Essay] How to Receive the 'Model Student Award'

Continuing from the "Education pt. VI" essay, it investigates how the school injects its morals into its students.


[The "Education pt. V" Fiction] How Excessive Education Drives Students Crazy

Revisiting all the issues addressed in the previous essays, it demonstrates, in the form of fiction, how problematic education can devastate students.
