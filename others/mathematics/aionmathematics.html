I have been attached to Mathematics for as long as I can remember. I liked to play with calendars on which I could do basic arithmetic when I was just one year old, and also liked to look at clocks for similar reasons when I was about three. And naturally, my mom, my schools, and the internet introduced me to various topics in Mathematics to study. However, I did not bother to consider whether what motivated me to study Mathematics was its innate beauty, its culture, the respect I recieved from others when I excelled in it, or my need for self-actualization.

I got the answer to this question when I entered the KSA. It was all of them. But, most of them were not major. Unlike most students in the KSA, I was closer to an artist than a sportsman. Although solving difficult problems in the limited amount of time on the exams excited me, it always did not last long enough to be the reason to study Mathematics. This is unfinished writing. Also, there were many students in the KSA who were more talented than me, and my skills were nothing to be respected. Coincidentally, AI began producing meaningful results in Mathematics in 2024, the year I entered the KSA, led by the release of OpenAI o1.

It meant I did not have to major in Mathematics to enjoy it, and I did not have to be a mathematician to appreciate it. I would like to introduce (again, I would not say "educate") Mathematics to people. That is why I started writing my version of Mathematics. However, this is not everything about Mathematics. I must admit that a wide range of applications account for a large part of Mathematics. But I hope that this can be a good starting point for people who are interested in Mathematics, and I hope that this can be a good reference for people who are already interested in Mathematics.
