This is unfinished writing.
I have liked Mathematics since before I can remember. I liked to play with calendars where I could do simple arithmetics when I was one year old. I liked to look at clocks for similar reasons when I was three or four years old. My mom, my schools, and the internet introduced me to various topics in Mathematics that I could explore (I am not sure if I can say "study"). However, I could not, or, I did not have to distinguish if what I liked about Mathematics was its innate beauty, or its useful applications, or respect from others.

I got an answer when I entered KSA. Unlike most students in KSA, I was closer to an artist than a sportsman. Coincidentally, AI began producing meaningful results in Mathematics in 2024, the year I entered KSA, led by the release of OpenAI o1.

It meant I did not have to major in Mathematics to enjoy it, and I did not have to be a mathematician to appreciate it. I would like to introduce (again, I would not say "educate") Mathematics to people. That is why I started writing my version of Mathematics. However, this is not everything about Mathematics. I must admit that a wide range of applications account for a large part of Mathematics. But I hope that this can be a good starting point for people who are interested in Mathematics, and I hope that this can be a good reference for people who are already interested in Mathematics.
